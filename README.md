## What is ACKO Gen?

ACKO Gen provides a web-based way to produce images, icons, and motion-oriented content. It is designed for people who prefer a direct creative process in the browser when preparing visual assets.

Rather than presenting a broad collection of unrelated tools, the project concentrates on a small group of generation tasks. This makes it suitable for early concepts, draft assets, and quick visual experiments while keeping the initial setup simple.
